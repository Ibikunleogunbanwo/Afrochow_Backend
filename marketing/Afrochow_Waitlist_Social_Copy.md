# Afrochow — Instagram & Facebook Copy (Waitlist Growth)

Goal: grow the customer waitlist ahead of launch. Every post drives to the waitlist sign-up (afrochow.ca/waitlist). Vendor registration is open separately — these posts are customer-facing.

Voice: warm, proud, direct and energetic — a friend who knows the best spots, not a corporate delivery app. Light emoji is on-brand; don't overdo it.

Posting notes: captions below work as-written on Facebook. For Instagram, keep the caption and drop hashtags into the first comment rather than the caption itself if you want a cleaner look — the hashtag set for each post is included.

---

**Visual:** Close-up, steaming bowl of jollof rice or egusi soup, dramatic overhead light.

**Caption:**
